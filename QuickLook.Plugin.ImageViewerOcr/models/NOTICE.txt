Detection and recognition models are ONNX exports published by RapidAI/RapidOCR
(converted from PaddleOCR):

- det.onnx: ch_PP-OCRv5_det_mobile
- rec_eslav.onnx: eslav_PP-OCRv5_rec_mobile (Russian, Ukrainian, Belarusian, English, digits)
- dict_eslav.txt: matching character dictionary

PaddleOCR models are distributed under the Apache License 2.0.
RapidOCR packaging follows that upstream license.
This plugin itself is GPL-3.0 because it links with QuickLook.
